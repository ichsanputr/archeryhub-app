Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. If you upgrade, the price will be prorated. If you downgrade, your new rate will start from the next billing cycle.

What happens after my free trial?

Your data remains safe. You'll be asked to choose a paid plan to continue adding scores, or you can stay on the Rookie plan with limited features.

Is there a discount for non-profits?

We offer special pricing for recognized national sports federations and youth archery charities. Contact our sales team for details.

How does Pay-as-you-go work?

You pay an initial setup fee, then you're billed per registered athlete once the event registration closes. No long-term commitment needed.
