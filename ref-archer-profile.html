Biography

Mete Gazoz is a Turkish recurve archer who won the gold medal in the men's individual event at the 2020 Summer Olympics. Known for his "rockstar" persona and clinical precision under pressure, Gazoz has become the face of modern archery. Since making his debut in 2013, he has consistently shattered world records and redefined the technical standards of the recurve bow.
